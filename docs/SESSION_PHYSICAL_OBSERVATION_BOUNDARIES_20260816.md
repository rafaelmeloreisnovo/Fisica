## 5. Quartz oscillator

Quartz is a piezoelectric resonator:

```text
voltage <-> deformation
```

with high-Q resonance. Typical watch quartz at 32768 Hz is not frequency-matched to the fundamental cardiac rhythm (~order 1 Hz). The valid comparison is only at the oscillator level:

```text
periodicity, phase, resonance, damping, stability
```
